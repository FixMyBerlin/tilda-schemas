Mapping targets become mapping choices: a kind of infrastructure (`MAPPING_KINDS`; the paths are one kind each instead of `_adjoining` and `_isolated`) plus options that do not exclude each other: `position`, `sign`, `lines`, `lifecycle`. `mappingChoiceOptions(choice, side)` says which options a kind offers (no sign under construction, no position and no second line on a road side, `temporary` only for some kinds); `mappingOptionRows` (the rows a form shows, with labels), `resolveMappingChoice`, `normalizeMappingChoice`, `mappingChoiceId`, `mappingChoiceLabel`, `mappingKindLabel`, `mappingChoiceForCategory`, `groupMappingKinds`, `allMappingChoices` and `MAPPING_OPTION_LABELS` go with it. `planForMappingChoice(tags, choice, side)` replaces `planForMappingTarget`; plans carry `choiceId` instead of `variantId` / `mappingTargetId`. Removed: `MAPPING_TARGETS`, `MAPPING_TARGET_LABELS`, `mappingTargetById`, `mappingTargetLabel`, `mappingTargetsForCategory`, `groupMappingTargets`, `isPlainMappingTarget`, `planCategoryVariants`, the types `MappingTarget` and `MappingTargetVariant`, and the conflict `unknown_mapping_target` (now `unknown_mapping_kind`).
