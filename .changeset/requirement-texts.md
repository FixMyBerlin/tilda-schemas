German and English texts for the required attributes, copied from the iD Radnetz Berlin editor: `ATTRIBUTE_LABELS` (a label for every attribute id, `ATTRIBUTE_IDS` lists them), the notes and hints (`ATTRIBUTE_NOTES`, `ATTRIBUTE_HINTS`), the reminders (`REMINDER_TEXTS`, title and text), the QA states (`QA_STATE_LABELS`) and the attribute states (`ATTRIBUTE_STATE_LABELS`, `ATTRIBUTE_STATE_TEXTS`). Helpers: `attributeLabel`, `attributeNote` (the sentence below an attribute: what TILDA makes of the tag, what a missing tag means), `attributeHint`, `attributeFeedsText`, `reminderText`, `qaStateLabel`, `attributeStateLabel`.
