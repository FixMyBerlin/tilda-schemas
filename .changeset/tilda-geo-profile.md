New profile `@tilda-geo/bicycle-infrastructure-tagging/profiles/tilda-geo` (`tildaGeo`): the required attributes TILDA itself reads, without a dataset (no infraVelo feeds, no reminders, one width for the whole of a segregated path). `RequirementsProfile` gets the optional fields `skipAttributes` and `partWidths` (default `true`, the Radnetz behaviour).
