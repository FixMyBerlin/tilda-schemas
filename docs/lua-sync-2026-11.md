## Riding direction (tilda-geo-private#34)

  `cycleway:<side>:oneway` are `implicit_yes` with the side default direction (right with the way, left against it;
  `oneway=yes` roads: both with the way; `oneway:bicycle=no` makes the left lane the contraflow lane; `oneway=-1` mirrored);

- Lua tests `derive_oneway.test.lua`, `bikelanes_oneway_direction.test.lua`, `transform_cycleway_opposite_schema.test.lua` became

### Merged on `develop` (2026-11-11)

`6ec40d600` and `bac701775` are what landed of the branch. Where they differ from the branch state above, the
package now follows `develop`:

- **`oneway` of a lane or track on the road without `cycleway:<side>:oneway`**: the category decides, like for own
  lines (`implicitOneWay`). Lanes and `shared_lane` stay `implicit_yes`; tracks and shared paths are `assumed_no` (the
  branch said `implicit_yes` for every side). The line direction still follows the wiki default per side.
- **Contraflow default dropped**: on a `oneway=yes` road with `oneway:bicycle=no` an untagged left lane follows the
  road, like on every one-way road (the branch followed the German mapping guide and made it the contraflow lane).
  A contraflow lane needs `cycleway:left:oneway=-1` or `cycleway=opposite_lane`. Same mirrored on `oneway=-1`.
  `defaultSideRunsAgainstWay` follows, so the tagging package suggests `yes` there when it extracts a side. The
  Radnetz rule `cycleway_oneway_wrong` keeps the contraflow case as an explicit Berlin heuristic.
- **Own lines (`_side=self`)** keep the drawing direction in `bikelanes`, also with `oneway=-1` or
  `oneway:bicycle=-1` (`_lineReversed=false`); only the routing reverses them.
- **Roundabouts** are one-way roads (`normalize_oneway_tags`): both sides follow the road, `cycleway=lane` is on
  the right and the left side is `not_expected`.
- **`unexpected_oneway_direction`** (branch, `5779b5419`): only `cycleway:*`, not `sidewalk:*`; a check date on the
  lane (`cycleway:<side>:check_date:oneway`, `cycleway:<side>:check_date`) confirms the direction.

## Other decisions

- **Units of `width`.** The task text said "cm/m/mm/ft/in as LUA does". LUA converts only `cm`, `m` and `km`; `mm`, `ft`, `in`, `mi` and
  everything else gives no width (`parse_length.test.lua` lists `2 mm`, `3 ft`, `6'` as unreadable). The package does what LUA does.
- **Todos are new in the package.** There was no todo code before. `src/derive/bikelane-todos.ts` ports every todo of
  `bikelane_todo_categories.lua` that only needs OSM tags. Left out: the `*__mapillary` variants and the priority (Mapillary pseudo
  tag), `currentness_too_old` (object timestamp), `adjoining_of_vs_csv` (sidepath CSV). The todos of a road (`road_todo_categories.lua`: `deprecated_cycleway_shared`, `unusual_width`) are in `src/derive/road-todos.ts`, with `fixtures/road-todos`.
- **`parking=no`** gives no traffic mode: the package already did that; now pinned by `fixtures/traffic-mode`.
- **Protective `separation` set** of `cyclewayOnHighwayProtected` (`bollard`, `flex_post`, `vertical_panel`, `studs`, `bump`, `planter`,
  `fence`, `jersey_barrier`, `guard_rail`): the package already matched; now pinned by category fixtures for each value, in and out.
- **Decision view.** LUA removed it (`df5a54bed`). The package keeps its Proxy, which behaves the same; see the table.

## Probable LUA bugs and questions

Settled 2026-11-05. Fixed in tilda-geo on `develop` (not pushed yet) and ported to the package, or checked and left as it is:

1. `missing_segregated` had `(object_tags.segregated ~= 'yes' or object_tags.segregated ~= 'no')`, which is always true.
   Now a set lookup, `not SEGREGATED_VALUES[object_tags.segregated]` (tilda-geo `ed8478350`). In practice the todo could not fire
   wrongly: with `segregated=yes|no` the category is no longer `needsClarification`.
2. `highway=cycleway` + `traffic_sign=none` (`98e649800`) also matched the side object of a lane between the car lanes ("Angstweiche",
   `cycleway:lanes=no|no|lane|no`) that is tagged on the road side as well (`cycleway:right:lane=advisory` + `cycleway:right:traffic_sign=none`).
   The three Berlin ways 1207199791, 529891428 and 1442781274 are all of this kind (Berlin extract of 2026-08-06). The unsigned
   case now has the guard `not has_cycleway_on_highway_between_lanes_conditions(tags)` like `needsClarification` (tilda-geo `34fb8457d`).
3. Oneway on the road sides: every lane on a road side without `cycleway:<side>:oneway` is `implicit_yes`, also
   `sharedMotorVehicleLane`. That is right: the processing splits a road into one object per side, and a `shared_lane` on a side is
   the car lane of that side, which runs in one direction. `cycleway:<side>:oneway=no` still gives `no`. The category flag
   `implicitOneWay = false` of `sharedMotorVehicleLane` only matters for ways of their own, which this category never is.
   On a `oneway=yes` road with `oneway:bicycle=no` the left side is the contraflow side, also for `shared_lane`. That is right:
   in Germany (extract of 2026-10-25) 107 ways have this, 63 of them with `shared_lane` only on the left (pictograms for the
   contraflow, mostly `cycleway:right=no`), 44 with `cycleway=shared_lane` or `cycleway:both=shared_lane` (pictograms for both
   directions on the one lane). Not changed.
   **Corrected 2026-11-11.** The `implicit_yes` for every side came from a rule of the PR branch that never reached `develop`; on
   `develop` a `shared_lane` was `assumed_no` before and after `6ec40d600`, because the category decides and
   `sharedMotorVehicleLane` had `implicitOneWay = false`. tilda-geo `0927094ca` (on `develop`, not pushed yet)
   sets `implicitOneWay = true`: every row of this category is one side of the road, also for `cycleway=shared_lane` without a
   side. The package has the same flag. The left side of a contraflow road follows the road (see
   [Merged on `develop`](#merged-on-develop-2026-11-11)).
4. `missing_access_tag_240` fired only when neither `bicycle` nor `foot` was `designated`. Now when one of them is missing (tilda-geo `61252e31f`, with tests).
5. `deprecated_cycleway_shared` read only `cycleway=shared`. Germany (extract of 2026-10-25) has 555 ways with `cycleway=shared`
